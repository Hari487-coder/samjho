# Your Liver, Explained

A working demo of a 3D explainer for patients. A doctor shares a patient's reports, and the patient gets a 3D model of their liver to turn with their hands, a narrated walkthrough, and an AI voice called Asha they can ask anything.

Open it here: https://hari487-coder.github.io/samjho/

- Rahul, the patient, and Dr. Ananya Rao are made up. The report numbers are demo data.
- The liver is a standard 3D model shaped by the report numbers. It is not a scan.
- Asha only explains the doctor's plan. She doesn't diagnose anyone or give medical advice.
- Hand tracking runs inside your browser, and the camera video stays on your device. When you talk to Asha, your voice goes to ElevenLabs to be answered.

Built with Three.js, MediaPipe hand tracking and ElevenLabs voice agents. Works best in Chrome on a laptop with a webcam.
